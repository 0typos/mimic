# Mimic brand

The mark shows one signal entering Mimic and leaving as two controlled,
fingerprint-aware presentations. Amber is the incoming request, cyan is the
selected presentation, and magenta is the comparison or compatibility variant.
The central hexagon is the proxy boundary where Mimic takes ownership of the
upstream connection.

## Assets

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `mimic-mark.png` | 512×512 master for the README, social cards, and project listings |
| `mimic-icon-128.png` | Compact avatar, launcher, or package icon |

Both files are opaque PNGs. Keep the deep-ink background intact; it is part of
the mark. Do not upscale the 512 px master.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| deep ink | `#071526` | background and high contrast |
| signal cyan | `#00CFE8` | selected identity and structure |
| input amber | `#FFB000` | incoming traffic |
| variant magenta | `#FF1688` | reproduced or comparison identity |

Use cyan as the dominant accent. Amber and magenta are signals, not decoration.
Pair the mark with the lowercase `mimic` name instead of baking a wordmark into
another image.
